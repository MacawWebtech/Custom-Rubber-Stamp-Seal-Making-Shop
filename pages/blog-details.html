How to read a stamp proof before you approve it

By Priya Natarajan, lead designer28 Sep 20266 min read

A proof is the last moment you can change anything for free. Once the laser has cut the rubber, a typo is permanent. Here are the four checks our designers run, and the ones we’d like you to run too.

1. Check the size at 100%

Proofs are sent as PDFs at true scale. Print at “actual size” and lay the sheet on the document you’ll stamp. Does it fit the space? A 58 mm stamp that looked small on screen can cover a whole invoice footer.

2. Look for lines that will fill in

Ink spreads slightly on paper. Lines thinner than 0.2 mm and gaps narrower than 0.3 mm tend to close up. We mark these in red on the proof; if you see a red note, decide whether to thicken or remove the detail.

3. Read every word backwards

Reading right to left stops your brain filling in what it expects. Check phone numbers digit by digit, and confirm suite numbers, postcodes and commission numbers against the original.

The most common reprint we make is a phone number with two digits swapped.

4. Confirm the ink colour

Screens can’t show ink exactly. If colour matters, for brand blue or a notary requirement, ask for a physical test card. It costs nothing and arrives in two days.
