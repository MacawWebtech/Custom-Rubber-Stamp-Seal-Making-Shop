    Artwork tips

    • Vector files (SVG, PDF) give the sharpest edges.
    • Raster images should be 300 dpi at final size.
    • Lines thinner than 0.2 mm may fill with ink; we’ll flag them.
    • Photos and gradients are converted to solid shapes.
