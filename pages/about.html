Anatomy of an impression

Five parts, one clean press

A stamp looks simple until it has to print the same crisp mark ten thousand times. Here is what we check on every part before it leaves the bench.

  1. 1

    The handle

    Turned from beech or moulded plastic, sized to sit in the palm so the pressure stays even.

  2. 2

    The body

    A flat, true face. If the body rocks, the impression fades on one side, so we test it on glass.

  3. 3

    The cushion mount

    A thin foam layer lets the die settle onto uneven paper instead of skipping across it.

  4. 4

    The ink

    Six standard colours plus custom mixes, matched to the paper it will print on.

  5. 5

    The impression

    Laser-cut rubber lines, test-printed and checked under a loupe before we pack it.
